---
title: Starting the journal
market: ES Futures
takeaway: Start the habit.
---
I've been teaching myself to trade since 2022. Now my focus is the part that matters most: following my rules the same way, every session. Writing each one down here is how I hold myself to it.

From here on, every trading day gets the same four steps: a plan before I trade, the session from 7:15 AM PT, a review graded on process, and an entry here. Some entries will just say I sat out because the market was ranging. That counts as following the plan.

If you're on the same road, come build this with me.
