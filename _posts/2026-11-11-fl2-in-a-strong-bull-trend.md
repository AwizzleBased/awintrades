### What happened
I originally planned a bigger position, but fear got to me. I thought the leg after bar 21 might have been a big enough spike to reset the leg count, which would mean this wasn't actually an FL2. Because of that doubt, I sized down to 1 micro.

The context was bullish. Price broke out of the opening range with follow-through, pullbacks were weak, and bars kept closing above the EMA.

I thought about trailing my stop, but that goes against my system and isn't something I usually do. I stuck to the plan and held to my target.

### Review
Overall, I'm glad I took the trade even though I was afraid.
