### What happened
Today was a reversal day. The market opened bullish and turned bearish in the second half, with several failed breakouts along the way.

The open broke below yesterday's low, but the breakout failed. Bulls quickly reversed up to yesterday's high, and that breakout failed too. The opening bull spike then became a final flag: a trading range just under yesterday's high, which acted as resistance.

Bears broke out of the range with follow-through and formed a bear spike and channel. The day ended with a wedge bottom.

### Review
I sat out today. I stayed patient and waited, and no setup met my rules. A clean day of following my system.
