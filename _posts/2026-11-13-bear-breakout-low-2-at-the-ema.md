### What happened
The day opened with a strong bear breakout below yesterday's low, with follow-through. The bear spike was big enough that traders expected at least a second leg down, so the first pullback to the EMA was likely to be sold. It formed a Low 2, and I sold below it.

The bears never got their second leg. The market went sideways and became a trading range, and I exited around breakeven. The bulls then broke above the range, and the rest of the day was a small bull channel into the close.

### Review
The context was bearish, and a big bear spike usually has a second leg, so the short was reasonable. I'm not upset that it didn't reach my target. No setup is guaranteed, and a reasonable trade can still fail. One trade, breakeven, and I followed my rules.
