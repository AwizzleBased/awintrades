### What happened
The day opened bearish. The bears got strong closes below several swing lows, including the Nov 13 and Nov 10 lows, so this was a bear breakout with follow-through.

The market then pulled back toward the EMA. The pullback made me think the market might be turning into a trading range. Instead, the bears made a new low. The bulls tried to reverse up at the prior low, which was a magnet, but their High 2 buy failed. A failed High 2 in a bear trend is a sell setup, and I sold below it.

### Review
I held to my target. During the trade I kept repeating to myself: trust my system. I'm really proud of today. Following my rules made me happier than the result did.
