### What happened
The day opened with a large bear spike. It started with a failed High 2 short, then broke through several lows, including the Nov 5 and Nov 6 lows.

After the spike, the bears had no follow-through. A deep pullback led to a failed breakout, and the market went sideways for the rest of the day in a contracting triangle. No trend, and no setup that met my rules.

### Review
I sat out today, but not by choice at the open. I lounged instead of getting to my charts on time, and I missed the failed High 2 short. I was upset about it, but I got over it quickly and stayed to watch for more signals. None came.

The missed trade isn't the lesson. Being late is. My routine starts before the open for a reason.
