### What happened
Today was a trading range. The market was waiting for the FOMC report at 11 AM, and most traders did not want to take big positions before it.

The bears tried to break below yesterday's low, and the bulls tried to break above the top of the range. Both breakouts failed. When breakouts keep failing, the market is in balance, and both bulls and bears are buying low and selling high.

### Review
I sat out. A trading range before a major report is not my kind of market, and staying out is part of my system. A clean day.
