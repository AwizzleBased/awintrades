### What happened
The market opened above yesterday's high, but there was no follow-through. The breakout failed, and the bears took over. They broke below yesterday's low, and the market pulled back toward the EMA.

The pullback formed a wedge top, which was a Low 2 sell setup. I was very hesitant. I thought it could become a Low 3 or a final flag, and bars closing around the EMA worried me. But the structure was still very bearish. The pullbacks were weak, and the bulls showed little strength. The Low 2 led to a large bear spike, and the market then went sideways into a trading range.

### Review
Fear got the best of me today, and I didn't take the trade live. I did take it on my paper account, and it reached my target. Looking back, the price action was telling me the market was bearish. Going forward, I want to follow the price action more and lean less on the EMA. Not my best day, but I spotted the setup and took it, even if only on paper.
