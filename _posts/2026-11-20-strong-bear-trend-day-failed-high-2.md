### What happened
I woke up early, did my morning routine, and was ready before the open. The market had been bearish all week, so I was prepared if today showed the same signs.

It did. The bears broke the opening range and the Nov 19 low with a breakout and follow-through. The bulls tried to push back up, and their High 2 failed. I sold below it without hesitating. The rest of the day was a strong bear trend day in a tight bear channel.

### Review
The setup and the entry followed my rules. The management did not.

My size was too large, and I felt it. Every tick brought anxiety and fear. As price moved toward my target, I was watching my P&L instead of the chart, and I closed out before the target was reached. I'm not proud of that exit.

The cause is clear: the size was bigger than I can hold calmly. On my next trade I'll size down, so I can let the trade work and hold to my target.
