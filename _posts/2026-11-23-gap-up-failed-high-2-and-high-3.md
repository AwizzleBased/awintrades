### What happened
The market opened with a large gap. The bears pulled back, and then the bulls made a new high. But the breakout had no follow-through, and the market then had a deep pullback to the EMA. Those were two signs that the bulls were getting weaker.

The pullback formed a High 2, and I bought it. It was a valid with-trend setup, but I didn't account for the signs of weakness, and I was stopped out. The market then formed a High 3. I bought that too, telling myself it was a High 2. It wasn't, and I was stopped out again. After the second loss, I stopped trading for the day.

### Review
I think I was still riding the high from two good weeks, and I felt unstoppable. I'm not. Today was humbling. The first trade was a valid setup that failed, but the second one broke my rules: when a High 2 fails, the bulls are telling me they are weak, and calling the next setup a High 2 doesn't change that. Context is king, and today I didn't respect it.

What I did right was stop after two losses instead of trying to win it back. Back-to-back losses, but a valuable lesson.
