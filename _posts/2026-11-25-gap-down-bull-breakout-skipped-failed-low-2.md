### What happened
The market opened with a large gap down and then went sideways in an opening trading range. The bulls broke out of the range with a strong spike and follow-through, and the market flipped to always in long.

The rally went up to yesterday's low, which was now resistance and a magnet. The market paused there for a few bars, and a possible Failed Low 2 formed. I didn't take it. The market then broke above yesterday's low and kept going up.

### Review
I skipped the Failed Low 2 for two reasons. First, the count: after the market made a second higher high, I thought the count might have reset, which would make this a Failed Low 1 at resistance, not a Failed Low 2. I wasn't sure, so I stayed out. Second, I was cautious because the market was testing yesterday's low.

But I have to be honest. I still feel the pressure from the last two days of losses, and I think part of me didn't want to take the trade because I'm afraid of losing again. The count was a real question, but the fear made the decision easier.

The context was clear: a strong breakout with follow-through, always in long, and the first test of resistance. In a strong bull trend, the first test of resistance usually only pauses the market before it breaks. When the count is unclear, the trend should decide.

On the next valid setup, I will trust my system, even if I trade it small. I need to let go and trust myself.
