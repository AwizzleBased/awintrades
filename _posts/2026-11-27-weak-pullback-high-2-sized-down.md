### What happened
A short session, the day after Thanksgiving. The market sold off after the open, and then the bulls came back with a strong spike up to the opening high. The breakout above it had no follow-through, but the pullback that followed was weak: a small two-legged move down to the EMA. The bears had a chance to reverse and didn't want it. Always in long.

The pullback formed a High 2 at the EMA, and I bought it. The market went up in a bull channel. I took my profit at 2R, and then watched the market make a new high above the 11/23 high.

### Review
This trade reminded me of 11/23, when I took a loss on a High 2. Both days the breakout had no follow-through. The difference was the pullback. On 11/23 it was deep, which told me the bulls were weak. Today it was weak, which told me the bears were weak. Same shape, different context.

I started the week heavy-handed, so today I sized down. I still felt fear coming into the trade, but the size was small enough that I didn't care if it won or lost. I felt no emotions during the trade. That is how I want trading to feel. Two days ago I wrote that I would trust my system on the next trade, even if small, and today I did.

One thing I'm not proud of. I chose to sleep in after spending the night with family, and some days that matters more. That was a choice, and I stand by it. But the way I woke up and went straight to my PC, messy and without my routine, is not how I want to start a trading day. Even on a late day, the routine comes first.
