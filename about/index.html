About

From practice to live.

I'm Awin, a price action trader in California. Awin Trades is my public trading journal, an honest record of every plan, trade and review as I take a system I've practiced for years and put it to work in live markets.

I found trading in high school through traders on YouTube. I didn't have a system back then, but I wanted a challenge, and trading seemed to have no ceiling at all. It made me feel special, like I was onto something most people would never try. Chasing that dream was the biggest high of my life, and I took risks without thinking twice.

In 2022 I discovered Al Brooks, and that's when I started learning price action properly. He talks about mastering one market before branching out, so I've spent my time on ES futures. Narrowing my focus made me calmer and more patient. Other markets will come later, but only once I've earned them.

Over the years my relationship with risk has changed. I'm more careful now, and I'd rather protect my account than chase a big day. That shift is a big part of why I feel ready for what comes next.

Where I'm at right now

I've run my setups thousands of times through backtesting and paper trading. I know them well. Knowing a setup is one thing, though. Trading it with real money, through good days and bad, without skipping the trades that scare you, is a different skill. That's the work in front of me now: building real belief in my system and staying with it through the emotions every session brings.

If I placed myself on the path to becoming a professional price action trader, I'd say I'm somewhere between learning from my mistakes and finding consistency.

Freedom comes with a price, and the price is self-discipline.

Two lessons that shape how I trade

  • LossesLosing trades are the most useful data I have. They show the emotions underneath a decision, and why a setup did or didn't work.
  • RepetitionConsistency means following your rules, every day. It's often boring. That's the point.

The morning I'm building

My goal is simple: be at the charts, calm and prepared, by 7:30.

  • FirstMake the bed
  • ThenHygiene, water and light stretching
  • Pre-marketPrep the charts and write the plan
  • 7:30 AMReady for the session

I'll be honest: I'm not there yet. Some mornings I get through most of it, and others I wake up and go straight to my PC, which is the habit I'm working to break. A good session starts long before the open, so this routine is part of the process too, and I'll track it in the journal like everything else.

Who this is for

Traders who want to share their work and learn from each other: beginners looking for a clear place to start, traders who feel stuck, and anyone who wants an honest conversation about the process. I'd like this to be a place where people get better together.
