How I Trade

Simple rules. Every day.

The same loop every trading day, whether yesterday was green or red.

  • Before 7:15 PTPlan. Key levels, the context, an if/then for both sides.
  • 7:15 PTExecute. Only the plan's setups. Stop at the daily limit.
  • AfterReview. Grade every trade on process, not the result.
  • Same dayJournal. Write it down and note one fix for tomorrow.

The rules

  • Rule 1Trade with the trend. Only the side the market is already on.
  • Rule 2Read the market cycle. Breakout, trend, range, repeat.
  • Rule 3Context is king. A setup only counts in the right context.
  • Rule 4Stay out of ranges. Sideways, overlapping bars mean no trade.
  • Rule 5One chart. The 5-minute. Price action is fractal.

Four setups

TrendPullbackFailed counter-trend
BullH2 buyFL2 buy
BearL2 sellFH2 sell

H2 / L2: the second leg of a pullback ends and the trend resumes. FH2 / FL2: the other side's entry fails, and I trade with the trend against them. These ideas come from Al Brooks' price action; I cover only the parts I use, in my own words.

  • Signal barThe bar that sets up the trade. I wait for it to close before I act.
  • Entry barThe next bar, the one that gets me in. For a buy, it has to trade above the signal bar's high. For a sell, below its low.

High 2 H2 · buy

A trend continuation buy. A weak two-legged pullback in a bull trend ends, and the trend resumes.

  • 1 · TrendBull trend. Price shows the structure and character of a bull trend.
  • 2 · PullbackWeak, two legs. The pullback is shallow and comes in two legs.
  • 3 · H1High 1. The first attempt to resume the trend fails.
  • 4 · H2High 2. The second attempt. Entry above the signal bar's high.
  • 5 · StopStop. Under the pullback low.

Why I take it

This is a trend continuation trade. The trend is already up, and the pullback is weak.

A High 2 works because the market tried to resume the trend once, failed, tried again, and is now showing that the trend is still strong enough to continue.

That is why I take it. I am buying a pullback in a bull trend, not picking a bottom. The odds favor the trend continuing.

Failed Low 2 FL2 · buy

A buy in a bull trend. The bears try twice to reverse it, and fail twice.

  • 1 · TrendBull trend. Price shows signs of a bull trend. Always in long.
  • 2 · FL1Failed Low 1. The first attempt down fails.
  • 3 · FL2Failed Low 2. The second attempt fails too. Entry above the signal bar's high.
  • 4 · StopStop. Under the pullback low.

Why I take it

In a bull trend the market is always in long. Every pullback is the bears trying to reverse it.

A Failed Low 2 means the bears tried twice and failed twice. After two failures, most bears give up. They buy back their shorts, and bulls who were waiting buy the pullback. Both sides are buying at the same time.

That is why I take it. I am not guessing at a reversal. I am trading with the trend, after the other side has shown twice that it cannot stop it. The odds favor the trend continuing.

Low 2 L2 · sell

A trend continuation sell. A weak two-legged rally in a bear trend ends, and the trend resumes down.

  • 1 · TrendBear trend. Price shows the structure and character of a bear trend. Always in short.
  • 2 · RallyWeak, two legs. The rally is shallow and comes in two legs, like a bear flag.
  • 3 · L1Low 1. The first attempt to resume the trend down fails.
  • 4 · L2Low 2. The second attempt. Entry below the signal bar's low.
  • 5 · StopStop. Above the rally high.

Why I take it

This is a trend continuation trade, the mirror of the High 2. Think of it as a bear flag forming inside a bear trend.

A Low 2 works because the market attempts to rally, fails to reverse the bear trend, and gives sellers another opportunity to push price lower.

That is why I take it. I am selling a rally in a bear trend, not picking a top. The odds favor the trend continuing.

Failed High 2 FH2 · sell

A sell in a bear trend. The bulls try twice to push price up, and fail twice.

  • 1 · TrendBear trend. Price shows signs of a bear trend. Always in short.
  • 2 · FH1Failed High 1. The first attempt up fails.
  • 3 · FH2Failed High 2. The second attempt fails too. Entry below the signal bar's low.
  • 4 · StopStop. Above the bounce high.

Why I take it

A Failed High 2 happens when buyers try to push price higher and the setup fails to produce the expected move up. Price fails to rally and reverses down instead.

Two groups then sell at the same time: bears who see that the bull setup failed and enter short, and bulls who bought and are now forced to exit.

That is why I take it. The odds favor the trend continuing.
